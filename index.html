datstr

A decentralized mining share network for Bitcoin and its BLAKE2b fork.

Miners build their own block templates, prove their work with Nostr-signed shares, and are paid directly in the coinbase. No custody, no mandatory fee, and every rule a verifier applies runs in a browser.

DATUM semantics, signed JSON wire, Solid documents, Nostr identity. Mining in the browser: a coordinator is a page with a socket, and a miner can be a tab.
