Pools should pool rewards,
not blocks.

datstr is a decentralized mining share network for Bitcoin and its BLAKE2b fork. Your node builds the block, a Nostr-signed share proves the work, and the coinbase pays you directly. No custody, no mandatory fee, and every rule a verifier applies runs in your browser.

How it works

Your template

The gateway runs beside your own node and builds every block from its getblocktemplate. Nothing north of the gateway ever sends a template, a transaction list or a merkle branch south. Your node's policy is your policy.

Your receipt

A share is the header, the coinbase and the merkle branches, signed by a worker key your did:nostr master delegated. The coordinator's signed ack makes it a receipt. What was acked and not paid is provable.

Your payout

The coordinator only dictates coinbase outputs, like DATUM's coinbaser, from a deterministic difficulty-summed window. Every block pays the window directly. Nobody ever holds the reward.

Four layers

1 · Bitcoin

Validity, settlement

btc and xbt and their testnet4s. A block is valid when your node accepts it, policy included.

2 · Share network

Accounting

Proof-of-work shares, a window, a split. Two verifiers with the same shares produce the same outputs, byte for byte.

3 · Nostr

Identity, discovery

did:nostr masters, delegated workers, signed documents. Relays carry them and are never the source of truth.

4 · Market

Optional, later

Variance transfer and claim trading are reserved names in the spec, not part of the protocol.

In your browser

Every check a datstr verifier makes is a bitcoin-kernel rule, and the kernel runs in a tab. So an auditor fetches the coordinator's documents, replays the shares, and checks the coinbase of every block against the ledger without trusting anyone. The same kernel can mine: a page with a WebSocket is a miner, and a coordinator is a JSS plugin, a page with a socket.

Friendly to DATUM

The model is DATUM's, on purpose: the miner's node builds the block, the pool only coordinates the reward. datstr keeps the semantics and the terms, and speaks signed JSON north of the gateway instead of DATUM's encrypted session. Existing hardware works unchanged, because stratum v1 stays on the gateway's south side, and a DATUM gateway is one adapter away from a datstr coordinator. With thanks to DATUM and to ratum.

What it stands on

  • Standardness is the default, not a filter the network imposes. Knots policy on the BLAKE2b chains, aimed at a monetary Bitcoin.
  • Fork-choice policy is out of scope. A verifier never refuses a share for what its parent contained.
  • Infrastructure is disposable. If every coordinator disappears, a gateway falls back to solo work and keeps its receipts.
  • If the coordinator cannot run on an old Android phone, the protocol is over-engineered.
